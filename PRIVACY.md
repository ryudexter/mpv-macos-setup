# MPV Player- Tiger Version: Privacy Policy

MPV Player- Tiger Version is a video player for the Mac. It does not collect,
store or share personal data, and it has no accounts, analytics or ads.

**Your videos stay on your Mac.** The app plays files you open and folders you
allow it into. What it remembers, such as where you stopped in each video and
the folders you allowed, stays in the app's own storage on your Mac.

**Find Subtitles Online (Premium).** Only when you press `b`, the app sends
OpenSubtitles.com the video's file name, a fingerprint of the file (a number
worked out from its size and its first and last 64 KB, which identifies the
release but contains none of the video), and the languages it wants, so it can
find a matching subtitle. The subtitle is saved next to the video. OpenSubtitles'
own [privacy policy](https://www.opensubtitles.com/en/privacy) covers what it
does with requests.

**Your OpenSubtitles account (optional).** If you sign in to OpenSubtitles in
Settings › Subtitles, for more downloads a day, the app sends your username
and password to OpenSubtitles.com and nowhere else. They are kept in your
Mac's keychain until you sign out, and the downloads you make then count
against your OpenSubtitles account.

**The iPhone remote.** The app lets the MPV Player Remote app on your iPhone
find and control it over your local network. Nothing goes over the internet:
the phone and the Mac talk directly, and paired phones are remembered on the Mac.

**Purchases.** Premium is bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment,
and the app only learns from the App Store whether Premium is active.
