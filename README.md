# MPV Player Tiger Version

A hand-tuned video player for the Mac, built on [mpv](https://mpv.io), coming
to the Mac App Store. Double-click a video and it plays: the whole folder
becomes the playlist, every file picks up where you left off, and the controls
stay out of the way until you reach for them.

This is its support page, and holds its [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).

## Quick start

### With the iPhone remote

[MPV Player Remote](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6823685362) turns your iPhone
into a remote for mpv on your Mac, over your home Wi-Fi. It works with either:

- **MPV Player Tiger Version**, coming to the Mac App Store, which has the
  remote's companion built in. Open a video, then open the remote on your
  iPhone and tap your Mac.
- **Plain mpv**, free from [GitHub](https://github.com/mpv-player/mpv/releases/latest)
  or [mpv.io](https://mpv.io/installation/), with the free MPV Remote script.
  [Setting it up](https://github.com/ryudexter/mpv-remote/blob/main/SUPPORT.md#setting-up)
  takes a minute, and there's nothing to buy.

Installed the earlier free setup with its one-line Terminal installer? It keeps
working as it is; the installer just isn't published any more.
